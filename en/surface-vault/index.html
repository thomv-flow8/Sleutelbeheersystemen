Key management systems

Surface Vault

One key or one key ring, behind a compact stainless steel vault on the wall. Your access control system or the Security Asset Manager releases it and sees whether the key is still in place.

How it works Models Features Specifications Dimensions Datasheet Questions

Monitored remotely, available on the spot.

The vault has no software of its own. It hangs off the system you already run and reports two things to it: whether the cover is open, and whether the key is still in place. Release happens from behind the desk or automatically on an alarm.

Remote release A relay output from your access control system, fire panel or the Security Asset Manager unlocks the door. An LED confirms it.
Door open or closed The door sensor passes on the state of the cover. If the vault is left unopened, it locks again after the time-out period.
Key present or gone The asset presence sensor reports whether the key is inside, so you can see from a distance whether it came back after use.

Models

One key or a whole ring.

Two models, the same sensors and the same way of connecting. The difference is what goes in and how the key is held.

Single Key Model For one key, held directly in an interchangeable core. The key only comes free once the system grants access.
  • Direct entrapment via interchangeable core
  • 5.9 × 20.7 × 5.4 cm
Key Ring Model For a bunch of up to fifteen keys. Holding is by cam lock, fob or direct entrapment; a loose access card fits in as well.
  • Cam lock, fob or direct entrapment
  • Ring for up to 15 keys
  • 10.9 × 19.4 × 6.7 cm

Features.

What the vault does, and what it deliberately does not need.

  • Connects to what you already have Access control, a fire panel, the Security Asset Manager or any device with a relay output that supplies enough power.
  • Door stays closed until released The user presses the door open once the system grants access. If the vault is not opened, it locks again after the time-out period.
  • Door sensor Reports remotely whether the cover is open or closed.
  • Asset presence sensor Reports remotely whether the key is in place or has been taken.
  • LED confirmation Illuminates as soon as the vault is unlocked and access is granted.
  • Mechanical key override With a cylinder, so the key stays reachable if the power fails.
  • No external fasteners Nothing on the outside to tamper with.
  • Surface mount On a wall or another suitable structure.

Specifications

Stainless steel, external power.

The housing is 16 gauge stainless steel with no fasteners on the outside. Power and control come from the system the vault is connected to.

Wherever a single key has to hang in a fixed place and be monitored remotely: an emergency key at the entrance, the lift key, the key to a meter cupboard or a plant room. For whole key stocks, look at the Keybox or the Security Asset Manager.
