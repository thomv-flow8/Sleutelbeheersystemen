KeyTracker key cabinets

Behind a door that closes itself. From 25 positions there is a matching steel cabinet with a self-closing door, mechanical code lock, emergency cylinder and four-point locking.

How it works Versions Options Sizes Questions

The board stays, the door is added.

Each cabinet takes one or two KeyTracker boards or hook panels: one inside the cabinet and one on the back of the door. Internal channels hold the board, so it slides in and out without tools.

The door falls shut behind the user. There is no moment at which the cabinet is accidentally left standing open.

Self-closing door Heavy-duty self-closing hinges pull the door shut. Whoever takes a key and walks on leaves a closed cabinet behind.
Four-point locking The door engages the case at four points. There is also an emergency cylinder, so the cabinet can be opened if the code is forgotten.
Code lock or your card reader A mechanical push-button code lock as standard: no power needed. Instead of that, an electric lock can be fitted, released by your own card reader.

Versions

Three cabinets, the same boards inside.

Each cabinet holds a maximum of two mechanical key systems or hook panels. The difference is in the lock and in how heavily the case is built.

Mechanical code lock The standard version: a steel cabinet with a self-closing door and a mechanical push-button code lock. Works without power and without a network.
  • Push-button code lock with emergency cylinder
  • Four-point locking
  • Left- or right-hand opening
Electronic lock The same cabinet, but with an electronic code lock or an electric lock released by your own card reader or access control.
  • Electronic code lock or card reader
  • Mechanical emergency cylinder remains
  • Connects to existing access control
High security The only police approved key cabinet from KeyTracker. Hardened reinforced steel at all entry points, with top and bottom armoured locks in a reinforced case.
  • Police approved
  • Hardened steel at all entry points
  • Two armoured locks, top and bottom

Options.

The cabinets are made to order. These are the choices you make when you enquire.

  • Support or plinth If the cabinet cannot be fixed to a solid wall, it stands on a steel support or plinth out in the room.
  • Mirrored front A mirror in the door lets the user see whether anyone is watching from behind while the code is entered.
  • Hand of the door Left- or right-hand opening, so the door swings to the side where you have the room.
  • Any colour A smart neutral powder coating as standard; any other colour can be ordered.
  • One or two boards One or two KeyTracker boards and hook panels: inside the cabinet and on the back of the door.
  • Internal channels Boards and panels slide into channels in the case, so fitting and removal needs no tools.

Sizes.

Four sizes, matching the KeyTracker boards from 25 to 150 positions. Width and depth stay the same; only the height grows with the number of positions.

For Cabinet h × w × d
KeyTracker 25 43 × 73 × 28 cm
KeyTracker 50 69 × 73 × 28 cm
KeyTracker 100 107 × 73 × 28 cm
KeyTracker 150 145 × 73 × 28 cm

Dimensions subject to change. The cabinets are made to order; if your space differs, we look at another size.
