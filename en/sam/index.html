What the cabinet records.

The cabinet decides who gets which key. Afterwards every action can be traced: who took what, when, and when it came back.

Identification per user Access by card, PIN code or fingerprint. Combinations are possible.
Only what is permitted Which positions are released is set per user or group. The rest stays locked.
Watertight registration Every issue and return with name and time, readable and reportable.

If a wing or a vehicle fleet is added, another cabinet goes beside it. The same operation, the same registration, one point of management.

  • Expand without replacing the existing cabinet

Configure.

Six elements can be chosen separately. What is possible for each is set out below.

Locks Doors Displays Access Identification Control

Locks

Every Security Asset Manager™ comes with KSI's own keys as standard. Locks hold the key firmly, cylinders are interchangeable, and the spacing between locks is generous enough for large objects and bunches of keys.

KSI Key Standard with every cabinet. The double-sided dimple design always fits — right way up or upside down, it goes straight in.
Direct Key Entrapment Holds a sensitive key in your own cylinder, safely stored away in the cabinet.
Interchangeable Cores Move or re-key your locks whenever you like. Small and large format cores available.

Doors

Three door versions, from solid steel to transparent Lexan — matched to what the cabinet holds.

Steel Door Solid 16-gauge steel. Standard, strong and fully shielded.
Lexan Door Transparent Lexan. One look and you see what is inside.
Expansion Door Need more positions? Simply added to your existing key cabinet.

Displays

Three ways to operate the cabinet: an integrated touch surface, a touchscreen or an external display.

Capacitance Solid touch surface without moving parts. Robust, responsive and water-resistant.
Touchscreen Display A large, bright screen. Operate everything at a glance.
External Touch Screen A separate touchscreen, mounted wherever you want it. Operation in your own spot.

Access

Five ways to open and unlock the cabinet, from a keypad to cloud management.

Keypad Enter your code and the cabinet opens. Immediately, no detours.
Net IP Connect the cabinet to your network and keep everything under central control.
Bluetooth™ Connect and open wirelessly, straight from your phone.
URL Manage everything through your browser, wherever you are.
Cloud Remote management. Anywhere, any time, securely.

Identification

Ten ways to identify users, from PIN code and card to fingerprint and iris.

Smartphone Your phone or tablet as a complete control panel: wireless entry, operation and biometric ID in one app.
Touchscreen A large, bright display. Operate everything at a glance.
Capacitance No moving parts. A solid Lexan surface: robust, responsive and water-resistant.
Proximity Access in one movement. Several protocols supported.
Card Swipe Familiar and fast. Swipe the card and you are in.
Biometrics You are the key. Effortless to set up, ready to use at once — as the primary identification or as an extra layer in two-factor authentication.
Maximum-Security Access A unique PIN code every time. Gone after use — nothing is stored.
Scale Weighs your asset immediately and reports every change to the SAM. It even spots a single key missing from the ring.
Fingerprint Reader Combines PIN code and fingerprint in one. GFMS stores every print securely in the user profile and shares it with all connected devices.
iT100 — Iris and facial recognition Contactless recognition of both iris and face, by Iris ID. From up to about 50 cm away with auto-tilt — even through glasses and masks.

Control

Five ways to drive the systems: your own server, the cloud, direct hardware or a link to existing access control.

GFMS™ Runs on your own server, your workstations or in the cloud. You choose where.
Direct Control Drive KSI equipment directly with hardware: lift control, PLCs and switches.
GFMS Integrator Seamless integration with access control from Lenel, AMAG, Software House, Genetec and more.
XML API Control your Security Asset Managers from your own software, through the KSI API's.
GFMS™ Sync Tool Merges data from your HR or access system with the GFMS database. Everything in sync.

Where SAM fits.

At sites where accountability is required: government, healthcare, data centres, property management and vehicle fleets. If not every action has to be recorded, often a Keybox of een Safebox.

Government Municipalities, provinces and public agencies: key issue that can be accounted for per person and per moment.
Hospitals and healthcare Medicine rooms, wards and emergency keys. Available on duty, closed outside it.
Data centres Rack and suite keys per customer or per cage, with a watertight log for audits.
Vehicle fleets Collect and return car keys 24/7, without a desk. Visible who has which vehicle.
Property management Keys to dozens of buildings in one place, issued to engineers and contractors.
Industry and logistics Machines, gates and forklifts: only authorised staff take the key.
Airports Airside doors, gates and vehicles. Issue tied to a valid authorisation, with a watertight log.
Energy and utilities Unmanned stations and remote installations: engineers collect the key where and when needed.

Frequently asked questions about the Security Asset Manager.

  • One cabinet runs from 8 to 96 positions, in five standard versions. If the number of keys grows, the cabinet does not have to be replaced: another goes beside it, with the same operation, the same registration and one point of management. Two cabinets side by side make up a maximum of 192 positions.

  • By card, PIN code or fingerprint as standard, and combinations of these are possible — for example card and PIN code for the sensitive positions. In total there are ten methods of identification, up to iris and facial recognition from about 50 cm away. The full overview is under Identification.

  • For every action: who took which key when, and when it was returned. Every issue and return is readable and reportable, so even months later an action can still be traced.

  • Yes. There are five methods of control, from your own server to cloud management and management through the browser. Which form suits you depends on your IT set-up; the overview is under Control.

  • You choose six elements separately: locks, doors, displays, access, identification and control. This page sets out the options for each — start at Configure.

  • At sites where accountability is required: government, healthcare, data centres and property management. Under Where SAM fits you will find the role of the cabinet in each environment.
