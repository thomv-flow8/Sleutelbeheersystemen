In use at

Key management at the level you need.

Some organisations simply want keys stored securely; others must be able to prove who took which key and when. DELPHI supplies every level, and a system grows with you as the requirement changes.

  • LEVEL 01 Storage Keys behind a lock, on a code or a key. No registration, but order.
  • LEVEL 02 Issue to authorised staff Who may take which key is set per person or per group.
  • LEVEL 03 Registration per key Electronic recognition of the ring: which key, who, how long.
  • LEVEL 04 Reporting and management An overview across several cabinets and sites, with alerts and reports.
