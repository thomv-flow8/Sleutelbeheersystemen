OC Spray Station

Issue OC spray, take it back and weigh it. The cabinet checks on return whether the canister is still to specified weight, and records per canister who held it and when.

How it works Features Cabinet sizes GFMS Questions

Weighing makes the difference.

A canister that has been half discharged weighs less but looks exactly like a full one. The internal scale evaluates every canister on return: back into service, or into the disposal section.

Canister is weighed The internal scale weighs the canister and evaluates whether it is to specified weight. So you know that what hangs in the cabinet actually works.
Issue on identification PIN code, card swipe, proximity card or fingerprint. Every canister is identified individually, so each transaction is tied to a name.
Disposal in its own section MK-4 cabinets have a separate disposal section. A canister that fails goes in there and is logged as out of service from that moment.

How issuing works.

From identification through return and weighing, filmed by the manufacturer.

Features.

What the cabinet does, and what you no longer have to track yourself.

  • Confirms canisters are loaded The canister is weighed by the internal scale and then evaluated for service or for disposal.
  • Reports per canister Canisters are individually identified. The software monitors usage, creates time zones and sends email alerts.
  • Four cabinet sizes MK-4 for 24, 48 or 80 canisters. MK-9 for 16 of the larger canisters.
  • User ID of your choice PIN, card swipe, proximity or biometrics.
  • Separate disposal section MK-4 cabinets include their own section for disposing of canisters.
  • Network ready Remote programming of access, with real time transactions to GFMS.

Cabinet sizes.

Four versions, the same controls. The MK-9 cabinet is intended for the larger canisters.
