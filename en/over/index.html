About DELPHI

We are not tied to a single brand.

That means a conversation starts with your situation, not with our range. Since 1996 we have supplied, installed and maintained key management in the Netherlands and Belgium — from a mechanical ring to an electronic cabinet with software.

Why DELPHI

What you get from us and not from a manufacturer.

  • Independent

    Not tied to a single brand

    We represent manufacturers in America, Sweden and England. If one system does not suit your situation, we propose the other — and that costs us nothing.

    Our manufacturers and partners ›
  • Every budget

    Several solutions, several prices

    From a mechanical board that works tomorrow to an electronic cabinet with software and reports. The same goal, different investments.

  • One point of contact

    From advice to maintenance

    Advice, supply, installation and the maintenance afterwards — with us, not spread across four parties. For installation we work with approved partners.

  • Complete

    The whole chain from one source

    Cabinets, lockers, storage systems, key rings and software. One party that knows how those parts fit together.

The complete package.

Key management is not a stand-alone product. Placing the ring, the cabinet, the issue and the registration with one party keeps things clear — and gives you one telephone number if anything happens.
