Vel-Key®

Securing and Organizing Keys Made Simple

Vel-Key® products increase security and productivity through simple effective key organization. Our plastic two-sided tags leave room for labeling one side and utilize durable Velcro® on the other for mounting in any one of our key organizers.

Products

Vel-Key® File

Sturdy plastic panel holds up to 22 Velcro® fastener-backed keys or 10 key card tags on a horizontal Velcro strip.

Zipper Case

A solution for organizing and keeping track of your keys while on the go.

Portable Key Organizer

Light weight plastic filing box that can hold up to 10 Vel-Key® files or 220 key tags in hanging folders.

One tag, every organizer

Each tag has a writable surface on one side and Velcro® on the other. The same tag therefore fits every Vel-Key® organizer: file, zipper case, cassette or portable organizer. The layout stays the same, even when keys move to another carrier.
