SAM lockers

Electronic Locker — Lockers of Every Size For Any Asset

Secured with controlled access. Assets electronically watched and there is power for charging. Audit trails on asset use and locker access.

General Features

  • Control and monitor access to individual lockers.
  • User ID Options: PIN, biometric, card, smartphone, touch free.
  • Monitor assets with barcodes, smart ID, and door assignment.
  • Assets can be designated as removed, returned, or defective.

Physical Feature Options

  • Doors available on front, back, or both for pass-through.
  • AC charging of stored assets available in each locker.
  • LED system lighting.
  • Wall and base support.
  • Engineering available to fit your requirements.

Any number of lockers

From a single cabinet to a full wall, with one control panel.

Every size

Layout and height follow the asset; LED system lighting.

Pass-through lockers

A door on both sides: one party deposits, the other collects.

SAM lockers · Operation

Operation Methods

Each locker system can be setup to operate in 5 different ways.

1

Door control

The door locks and unlocks only. What is recorded is who opened which locker, not what was inside.

2

User designated asset management

Asset management as designated by the user: on return the user states whether the asset is returned, still removed, or defective.

3

Barcode asset management

Asset management with barcodes. Scanning on issue and return records which item was taken.

4

Electronic Smart ID asset management

Asset management with an electronic identity: the asset is identified in the locker and continuously monitored.

5

Self-Serve

Simple operation for visitors and occasional users, without prior instruction.

Direct Control

The Electronic Locker is also available as a Direct Control version: without the embedded electronics, driven by your own access control system.

SAM lockers · Applications

What goes into the lockers

Layout, height, ventilation and power supply are determined per application.

Two-way radios

Charging while stored, with a socket and charging dock in the locker.

Laptops

Lockers sized to the device, with AC power and perforated doors for heat.

Tablets

Several devices per locker, all on charge.

Weapons

Issue and return recorded per person, with an audit trail per locker.

Ammunition

Drawers with a fixed layout, keeping quantities auditable.

Medical equipment

Instruments under lock, with a record of who took them.

Evidence

A pass-through locker keeps the chain of custody demonstrable: deposit on one side, collect on the other.

Tools and other assets

Assets can be designated as removed, returned, or defective.
