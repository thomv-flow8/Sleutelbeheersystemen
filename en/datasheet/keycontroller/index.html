Chit-Key™ Vault and Panels — Simple in Design, Superior in Performance

Chit-Key Vaults™ and Panels provide a simple and clever technique to ensure the return of keys. All locks work in tandem so that the two keys may never be released at the same time. Securing the first position (control key) in place, the second position (user key) is mechanically free to be removed. The control key is then locked in place until the safe return of the user key.

Chit Key Vaults™ come in two sizes of 18-gauge brushed stainless steel. These vaults may be customized to any size with a variety of lock options to meet your requirements.

Versions

1 Tandem Position

Also available as 1 Tandem Position XL – Horizontal.

Six-Position Chit-Key Vault

This Chit-Vault™ holds six tandem locks. These locks may optionally be mastered to control the front door of the Chit-Vault™. Common pinning the door lock and interior locks is optional.

The Multi-Chit Panel

Multi-Chit can hold up to 50 Chit positions at one time. Mounted stand alone or housed in a SAM with optional electrical door control.
